# Olaf Zen

The wallpaper app that doesn't change your wallpaper. Olaf Zen lays your pictures over
the desktop: one picture across all your monitors, or one per display, with a scene for
every situation. macOS stays exactly as it was, and it also works on a Mac where the
company locks the wallpaper.

**Download and install: [olafzen.com](https://olafzen.com)**

## Wallpaper locked on a work Mac?

Many companies set the wallpaper with an MDM profile (Intune, Jamf and others), and
then it can't be changed in System Settings. Olaf Zen wasn't made to get around that,
and it doesn't unlock or change it. It works the same on every Mac: it draws your
picture on a layer over the desktop. On a managed Mac the company's setting stays
untouched underneath, and switching Zen Mode off brings the company look back at once.
Ask IT if you're unsure, and if your company says no, the answer is no.
More: [Wallpaper locked on your work Mac?](https://olafzen.com/go/github/locked)
